Email Notifications configured via .env

Set via EMAIL_PROVIDER in backend/.env: custom (your own self-hosted relay - no third-party ESP needed), mailjet (free forever, works immediately), brevo (free forever but needs a 1-2 day manual approval first), sendgrid (now a 60-day trial only), or smtp (local dev / paid Render plans - Render's free tier blocks outbound SMTP entirely). Notifications are sent for: new matching jobs found by the daily search, and applications due for a follow-up.
